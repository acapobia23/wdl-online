Park2Go Florence – The Ideal Parking Solution for Guests next to the Duomo!
Park2Go Florence is located in the historic center of Florence, in Via Ricasoli 18,
just 80 meters from the Florence Cathedral (Duomo), making it the ideal starting point for exploring the city on foot.
It is a secure and monitored garage with authorized ZTL access and spacious, practical parking spaces.

The perfect solution for guests arriving by car who want to enjoy Florence stress-free.




The garage is located inside the Limited Traffic Zone (ZTL) of Florence.


Important:

ZTL access is managed directly by the garage.
Upon arrival, the staff will electronically register your vehicle plate number with the Municipality of Florence, authorizing your access to the ZTL area. The municipal access pass costs €1 and is included in the parking fee.

Once you enter the ZTL area, you have a maximum of 3 hours to reach the garage.
If you leave and re-enter the ZTL during your stay, a new plate registration will be required (€1 extra for each new access).
The authorization only applies to ZTL access and does not include bus lanes, pedestrian areas, or restricted emergency/service lanes.
