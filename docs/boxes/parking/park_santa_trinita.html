BOOK YOUR PARKING

In an excellent garage with ZTL access in the heart of Florence, Garage Santa Trinita offers a professional valet-style parking service in the historic center of Florence. Upon arrival, the staff will take charge of your vehicle and park it for you. For this reason, you will be asked to leave your keys, which will be securely stored by the staff.

The garage is protected by a 24-hour CCTV security system, ensuring the safety of your vehicle throughout your stay.
