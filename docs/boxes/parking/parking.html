Parking in Florence


Parking in Florence requires some attention, as the historic city center is regulated by the ZTL – Limited Traffic Zone.

Below you will find all the recommended solutions to arrive and park without risking fines.



Official website:
https://www.fipark.com/


These parking facilities are:

  • Covered
  • Monitored
  • Paid by the hour
  • In some cases with a maximum daily rate

Note on rates


Not all parking facilities have a fixed daily rate.
Many apply an hourly rate with a daily maximum cap.
Up-to-date rates are always available on the individual parking pages on fipark.com.

Official website:
https://www.serviziallastrada.it/


In Florence, street parking is regulated by the color of the road markings and by vertical signs.
Vertical signage always has priority.


Blue lines


  • Always paid parking
  • Valid for everyone
  • Payment via parking meter or app
  • Typical rates in the city center (ZCS 1): €2.40 for the first hour, €3.00 per hour thereafter
  • Typical rates in semi-central/residential zones (ZCS 2–5): €1 for the first hour, €2 per hour thereafter
  • Rates may vary slightly based on local signage and time of day
  • Paid hours usually: Monday–Saturday daytime
  • Even with ZTL permits, blue lines are always paid

White or yellow lines


  • Reserved for residents
  • White lines are gradually being replaced by yellow lines
  • The meaning is the same
  • Always check vertical signage: if it indicates "residents only" or "ZCS", parking is prohibited, even for tourists with ZTL permits
  • White/yellow spaces without vertical signage exist but are very rare in the historic center
  • With a "Transit + Parking" ZTL permit, tourists can park legally and free of charge in white-line spaces where there is no ZCS sign

In practice, in the historic center, street parking is almost always paid or restricted, so tourists will often use blue lines or covered parking.



To enter the central area by car, the following options are available:


ZTL transit only


  • Approximate cost: €5
  • Allows only transit through the ZTL
  • Does not allow street parking

ZTL transit + street parking


  • Approximate cost: €15
  • Allows:
    • Access to the ZTL
    • Parking in white-line spaces reserved for residents where vertical signage allows it
    • Blue-line parking remains always paid

To request temporary ZTL access and parking for tourists, use the official website:
https://www.serviziallastrada.it/servizi-al-turista/accessi-temporanei-ztl


Summary of street parking rules with this permit:


Line type Vertical sign Access for tourists with "Transit + Parking" permit
Blue any Always paid
White none Free with permit
White ZCS / residents Not allowed
Yellow any Not allowed


There are private, guarded, covered parking garages in the city center.
They are generally more expensive, but:

  • They have a ZTL agreement
  • They allow access to the ZTL without purchasing separate permits
  • They offer greater convenience
