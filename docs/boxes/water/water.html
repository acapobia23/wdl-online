💧 Drinking Water in Florence

Tap Water in Florence – Safe, Tasty & Free


Yes, the tap water in Florence is perfectly safe to drink.
It’s treated according to strict EU standards with clarification, filtration, and disinfection (ozone and chlorine dioxide).

✅ Safety and quality

The water is constantly monitored.
ARPAT and Publiacqua test it regularly, and Florence consistently meets all legal standards.
Italian tap water ranks among the top in Europe, placing 5th in a recent ranking.

💧 Taste and smell

Some notice a slight sulfur-like smell due to natural minerals – harmless and varying by area.
If water is not safe to drink, clear signage will indicate it.

🚰 Local tip: luxury fountains

Florentines often use public fountains with purified, refrigerated still or sparkling water – free of charge.
Bring reusable bottles or jugs.
If someone ahead has many containers, it’s polite to ask if you can go first – people are usually friendly.

⚠️ Note

On very hot days, heavy use can mean water isn’t fully chilled.
Other non-luxury fountains exist with drinkable but unfiltered water – search “fontanello” on Google Maps.

📌 Tips

At restaurants, you can ask for tap water (“acqua del rubinetto”), but it’s not always served in pitchers – sometimes just in a glass.
Bring a reusable bottle to save money and reduce plastic waste.

Stay hydrated like a local and enjoy Florence’s high-quality water for free.
