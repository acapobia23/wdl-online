Vino Divino – A Taste of Traditional Florence

Discover one of Firenze's authentic traditional wine shops, where the old vino sfuso tradition is still alive. Instead of heading to the supermarket, stop by and experience a local tradition.

You can buy a reusable bottle for around €1.60 and fill it with your choice of wine from the tap. A bottle filled with wine costs around €3, making it an affordable way to enjoy good, local wine during your stay.

There is a selection of wines on tap and carefully chosen bag-in-box wines, offering plenty of options to suit different tastes and budgets. Fancy a glass right there? You can sit down and enjoy a glass of chilled white or red wine for around €2.50.

Places offering this traditional refill service are becoming increasingly rare in Florence, making Vino Divino a lovely discovery for anyone who wants to experience the city beyond its usual tourist spots. It's right next to the Mercato di San Lorenzo, very central.

So, a simple, authentic and affordable experience: good wine, local tradition and excellent value.
