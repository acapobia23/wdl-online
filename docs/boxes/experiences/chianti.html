Chianti Pasta Lesson

Discover the flavors of Tuscany with a private pasta-making class in the heart of the Chianti countryside. Surrounded by rolling hills and vineyards, this experience blends local tradition, fresh ingredients, and the beauty of rural Italy.

What’s Included: Hands-on pasta workshop with a professional chef, your choice of two traditional pasta dishes (including options like Ravioli with truffle oil or Fettuccine Alfredo), homemade dessert (Tiramisù or Chocolate Salami), local Chianti wine, and a taste of homemade limoncello.

Location: Private villas and estates in the Chianti region.

Pricing (up to 10 participants): €900 total
Larger groups: price on request
