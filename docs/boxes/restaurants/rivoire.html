Rivoire


One of Florence's most iconic cafés, Rivoire has overlooked Piazza della Signoria since 1872. Founded by Enrico Rivoire, chocolatier to the Royal House of Savoy, it remains one of Florence's most elegant places for breakfast, lunch, aperitivo and snacks.

Rivoire is also closely linked to Florentine cocktail history: it was one of the favorite cafés of Count Camillo Negroni, credited with inventing the world-famous Negroni cocktail just a few steps away in Piazza della Signoria.
Famous for its artisanal hot chocolate, homemade pastries and refined Italian cuisine, Rivoire combines history with one of the city's most spectacular terraces overlooking Palazzo Vecchio.



Breakfast (8:00 AM – 11:30 AM)


Choose between an Italian Breakfast at the Bar (espresso or cappuccino with a fresh croissant), an Italian Breakfast at the Table, or an International / English Breakfast at the Table.
No reservation required — seating is first-come, first-served.
English breakfasts are available until 11:30 AM, while croissants, espresso, cappuccino and other café specialties are available throughout the day.


Lunch (12:00 PM – 5:00 PM)


Reservations are recommended — lunch is the only service that accepts reservations.
Signature dishes include Pappardelle with Wild Boar Ragù, Pici all'Aglione, Beef Peposo, Florentine Steak, Sea Bass, and a Tuscan Charcuterie & Cheese Board.


Tapas & Cocktails (5:00 PM – 10:00 PM)


Enjoy signature cocktails, tapas and one of the most beautiful terraces in Florence overlooking Piazza della Signoria.
No reservation required. The tapas menu is available throughout the day.
