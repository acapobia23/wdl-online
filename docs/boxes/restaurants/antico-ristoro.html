All'Antico Ristoro di' Cambi


If you're looking to try a true Florentine Steak, All'Antico Ristoro di' Cambi is one of the restaurants we recommend the most.

Established in 1950, this historic restaurant welcomes both locals and visitors in the heart of San Frediano, one of Florence's most authentic neighborhoods. What started as a traditional local tavern has become one of the city's landmarks for Tuscan cuisine while preserving the warm atmosphere of a classic Florentine trattoria.



What to Expect


The Florentine Steak (Bistecca alla Fiorentina) is the undisputed star of the menu. Cooked according to local tradition and priced at €60 per kg, it represents a main reason for visiting this historic spot. If this is your first Florentine Steak, there is one important custom to keep in mind: an authentic steak is traditionally served rare and is cut at least four fingers thick to keep the meat tender and juicy inside.

Local Tip


As a historic landmark in Florence, tables at di' Cambi fill up quickly, especially during weekends and dinner hours. Planning ahead and securing a reservation is highly recommended.

If you prefer your Florentine Steak prepared medium or cooked further rather than the traditional rare style, simply let the staff know; the kitchen team at Cambi will prepare it to your preference upon request.
