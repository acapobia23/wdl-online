Weekly Tips

TIPS FOR THE WEEKEND 7th - 8th NOVEMBER IN FLORENCE




Museum recommendations: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting Broken, a major exhibition exploring the theme of fragments in the history of sculpture, from archaeology to contemporary art. At the Cattedrale dell'Immagine, you can experience Inferno, an immersive exhibition dedicated to Dante Alighieri. Finally, Villa Bardini is hosting Firenze '50 '60 '70, a photographic journey through three decades of Florence's history, told through images from the Foto Locchi Archive. The Museo degli Innocenti has just opened a major exhibition dedicated to Artemisia Gentileschi, the first woman admitted to the Academy of Arts. She received prestigious commissions from the Medici family and was part of the same circles as Galileo Galilei and other artists of her time.

Textile Arts Fair: this weekend, Piazza Santa Croce will host a market dedicated to locally sourced wool and yarns, with plenty of shopping opportunities, workshops and live demonstrations (9:30 am-7 pm).

Artefacendo: on Saturday, Piazza dei Ciompi will come alive with an arts and crafts market featuring a variety of exhibitors showcasing their handmade creations. A great opportunity for locals and visitors to discover and buy unique artisan products and meet the people behind them (10 am-8 pm).

Florence Vintage Market: starting Friday, Palazzo Corsini will host a charity vintage market featuring clothing and accessories from past eras (11 am-6 pm).

Smile Market: starting Friday, head to Manifattura Tabacchi for this charity shopping event, featuring new clothing, shoes, bags and accessories from major designer brands. A great mix of fashion, big names and giving back (9 am-7 pm).

Fierucola del Carmine: on Sunday, Piazza del Carmine is the place to go for your weekly food shopping, with local producers offering everything from bread, pasta and fresh vegetables to new-season olive oil, wine, fruit, honey, flour and cheese (8 am-1 pm).

Arts and Crafts of Oltrarno: on Sunday, the square will host a lively market with plenty of stalls selling handmade crafts, antiques, books, clothing and vintage finds, as well as local specialities such as honey and truffles, alongside artistic and ethnic crafts (9 am-7 pm).
