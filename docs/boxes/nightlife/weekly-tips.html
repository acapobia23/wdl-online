Weekly Tips




THE WEEKEND 14 - 15th MARCH IN FLORENCE




MUSEUMS, EVENTS and MARKETS:


Rothko in Florence: The Fondazione Palazzo Strozzi is presenting an unmissable exhibition dedicated to the great American art master Mark Rothko. The show explores the evolution of his work: from his early figurative paintings, influenced by Expressionism and Surrealism, to the famous abstract canvases of the 1950s and 1960s, also highlighting his connection with the Italian artistic tradition.

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec: The Museo degli Innocenti takes you on an immersive journey into fin-de-siècle Paris, featuring iconic posters, colorful lithographs, and illustrations by the French master Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec.

Oceani. David Doubilet: Villa Bardini and National Geographic celebrate the work of photographer David Doubilet, a pioneer of underwater exploration and one of the world's leading underwater photographers.

Frida Kahlo, Emotion Exhibition: The Cattedrale dell'Immagine hosts an exclusive immersive exhibition dedicated to the incredible Frida Kahlo, letting visitors dive into the rich and complex universe of the iconic Mexican artist.


Cioko Flo: Master chocolatiers and pastry chefs from Italy and around the world will transform Piazza Santa Croce into a celebration of high-quality artisanal chocolate, with tastings, direct sales, workshops, and installations (9am-8pm).

Rare Market: This weekend the Ippodromo del Visarno hosts a market where you can discover vintage and handmade treasures. Expect good food, great music, and workshops while browsing through stands full of unique finds (11am-8pm).

Ognissanti in Festa: On Saturday, head to Piazza Ognissanti for a market exhibition dedicated to vintage collecting and creative crafts (9am-8pm).

Indipendenza Antiquaria: Over the weekend, stop by Piazza Indipendenza and browse stands filled with curiosities, antiques, and objects from the past (9am-7pm).

Creative Factory: A weekend in Piazza dei Ciompi dedicated to the beauty of local handmade production. You'll find ceramics, textiles, illustrations, jewelry, accessories, and decorations (10am-8pm).

Ars Manualis: This weekend in Piazza Santa Maria Novella you'll find an artistic crafts market (10am-7pm).

Only Usato Market: Spend your Sunday afternoon shopping at the Limonaia di Villa Strozzi, where you'll find vintage and second-hand clothing and home goods (3pm-11pm).

Fiera dei Quaresimali: The countdown to Easter begins with the Quaresimali fairs, which for four Sundays will fill Parco delle Cascine with hundreds of stalls stretching for about a kilometer and a half (8am-8pm).

Fierucola di Santo Spirito: On Sunday, head to Piazza Santo Spirito to buy local farm products and handmade crafts — an opportunity for shopping that ranges from artisanal goods to organic agriculture (9am-7pm).
