Weekly Tips

TIPS FOR THE WEEKEND 10-11th OCTOBER IN FLORENCE




EVENTS:

Middle East Now: a film festival dedicated to Middle Eastern culture, held at Cinema La Compagnia, featuring over 30 films and documentaries from Palestine, Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Egypt, Morocco, Afghanistan, Saudi Arabia and Sudan

Firenze Book: at the Visarno Racecourse, independent bookstores will come together for a lively event packed with talks, events, food and, of course, lots and lots of books!

FAI Autumn Days: a chance to explore historic sites, military institutions, villages and hidden treasures that are usually closed to the public. Reservations at fondoambiente.it

MUSEUM RECOMMENDATIONS:

Broken: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting a major exhibition exploring the theme of fragments in the history of sculpture, from archaeology to contemporary art. At the Cattedrale dell'Immagine, you can experience Inferno, an immersive exhibition dedicated to Dante Alighieri. Finally, Villa Bardini is hosting Firenze '50 '60 '70, a photographic journey through three decades of Florence's history, told through images from the Foto Locchi Archive

MARKETS:

Firenze Flower Show: this weekend, Giardino Corsini will welcome some of Italy's best plant growers and artisans, showcasing everything from carnivorous plants and orchids to bonsai and Japanese maples, as well as roses, citrus trees and tropical varieties that are hard to find in regular shops

Creative Factory: over the weekend, Piazza dei Ciompi comes alive with a selection of artisans, designers and makers for a celebration of creativity and all things handmade (H 9:30/20)

Ognissanti in Festa: on Saturday, the square of the same name will host a market dedicated to vintage collectibles and creative crafts. A chance to find unique, one-of-a-kind pieces while taking a trip down memory lane and discovering some great handmade creations (H 9/20)

Fierucola del Carmine: head to Piazza del Carmine to do your shopping at this farmers' market, featuring products from local small-scale producers, including bread, pasta, vegetables, new-season olive oil, wine, fruit, honey, flour and cheese (H 8/13)

Fiera d'Oltrarno: on Sunday, the piazza will be filled with stalls selling crafts, antiques, books, clothing and vintage finds, as well as local specialties such as honey, truffles and much more (H 9/19)

Only Usato: on Saturday at Giardino dell'Orticultura and Sunday at Conventino, check out this event dedicated to reuse and second-hand shopping, with clothes, accessories, vinyl records and books, plus workshops, food, music and much more (H 11/20)

DegustaVino: on Sunday, Via Gioberti will celebrate quality wine, with over 100 wineries, producers from the Gallo Nero area and plenty of local neighborhood vibes (H 12/19:30)

Fiera di Scandicci: the final weekend of this huge fair, with over 300 exhibitors offering crafts, shopping, street food, local specialties, culture and entertainment

The Kingdom of Pumpkins and Straw: until November 1st, the autumn season returns to Parco di Villa Montalvo in Campi Bisenzio, with giant pumpkins, huge straw sculptures, shows, workshops and a whole amusement park (H 10/20)
