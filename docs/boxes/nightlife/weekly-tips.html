Weekly Tips




The Weekend 21-22 February in Florence




🎨 Events / Museums

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec: Museo degli Innocenti takes you on an immersive journey into fin-de-siècle Paris,
featuring iconic posters, colorful lithographs, and illustrations by the French master Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec.

Oceani. David Doubilet: At Villa Bardini, National Geographic arrives for the first time to celebrate the work
of photographer David Doubilet in Italy, a pioneer of underwater exploration
and one of the world's leading figures in underwater photography.

Frida Kahlo, Emotion Exhibition: Cattedrale dell'Immagine hosts this exclusive immersive exhibition
dedicated to the incredible Frida Kahlo, giving visitors the chance to dive into the rich and complex universe
of the iconic Mexican artist.



🛍️ Markets and Food

Vintage Market: In Piazza dei Ciompi you'll find a market full of clothing, comics, records
and all things vintage (H 9/20).

Santo Spirito Fair: On Sunday, head to Piazza Santo Spirito for stalls selling handmade goods,
small antiques, clothes, costume jewelry, sweets, wellness products, gift ideas, cheese, honey and more (H 8/20).

Only Usato Market: On Sunday, stop by the Limonaia at Villa Strozzi and check out this market
all about second-hand and vintage culture.

Collezionare a Firenze: This weekend at the parking area of Teatro Cartiere Carrara (formerly Tuscany Hall),
the collectibles fair is back with small antiques and modern collectibles (H 10/19).

Local Food Festivals: Around Florence you can find the "Sagra del Bollito" in Pelago,
the "Polenta and Winter Flavors Festival" in Sieci, and the Chocolate Festival in Empoli.

Carnival Around Florence: On Sunday, lots of towns will dress up to celebrate Carnival!
Go see the amazing floats in Viareggio, the floats and masks in San Gimignano,
or the marionettes in Fiesole.
