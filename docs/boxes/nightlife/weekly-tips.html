Weekly Tips

TIPS FOR THE WEEKEND 24 - 25th OCTOBER IN FLORENCE




MUSEUM RECOMMENDATIONS:

Broken: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting a major exhibition exploring the theme of fragments in the history of sculpture, from archaeology to contemporary art.

Inferno: at the Cattedrale dell'Immagine, experience an immersive exhibition dedicated to Dante Alighieri.

Firenze '50 '60 '70: Villa Bardini is hosting a photographic journey through three decades of Florence's history, told through images from the Foto Locchi Archive.

Artemisia Gentileschi: the Museo degli Innocenti has just opened a major exhibition dedicated to the first woman admitted to the Academy of Arts. She received prestigious commissions from the Medici family and was part of the same circles as Galileo Galilei and other artists of her time.

FESTIVALS AND MARKETS:

Festival delle Regioni: spread across Piazza Santa Croce, Piazza Duomo and Piazza della Signoria, turning the three squares into lively meeting places for promotion and community engagement, with dedicated areas for technology, culture, health, sports and local products.

East Market: this weekend, Manifattura Tabacchi will host over 100 exhibitors showcasing thousands of items, from vintage clothing and design pieces to vinyl records, accessories and collectibles (H 10/23).

Fiera di Santo Spirito: on Sunday, Piazza Santo Spirito will fill up with stalls selling crafts, antiques, books, clothing and vintage finds, as well as local specialties such as honey, truffles and much more (H 9/19).

Fumetti e dintorni: this weekend, Piazza dei Ciompi will host a market celebrating all things retro, from comics and records to vintage finds. Music lovers can browse everything from vinyl and CDs to cassette tapes (H 9/20).

Rionalissima: Florence's big autumn street market is back this Sunday, bringing a huge selection of stalls to the streets around the Artemio Franchi Stadium.

Festival delle Streghe: the Visarno Racecourse will turn into an enchanted village for two days celebrating mystery, magic and folk traditions. The program includes shows, talks, workshops for kids, performances and themed markets (H 11/20).

Il regno delle zucche e della paglia: until November 1, the autumn festival returns to Villa Montalvo Park in Campi Bisenzio, featuring pumpkins, giant straw sculptures, shows, workshops and even a whole amusement park (H 10/20).

Paint and Drink: in the beautiful setting of Villa Strozzi Machiavelli, enjoy a glass of wine while creating your own painting, guided by artist Enrico Guerrini (info: +39 347311903).
