Weekly Tips

THE WEEKEND 27 – 28th NOVEMBER IN FLORENCE




- Museum Tips: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting Broken, a major exhibition exploring the theme of fragments throughout the history of sculpture, from ancient archaeology to contemporary art. At the Cathedral of the Image, you can experience Inferno, an immersive exhibition dedicated to Dante Alighieri. The Museo del Novecento, with its exhibition Stimmung, takes you on a journey through paintings, sculptures, atmospheres and soundscapes, offering a whole new way of experiencing art. And finally, the Museo degli Innocenti is hosting a major exhibition dedicated to Artemisia Gentileschi – the first woman to be admitted to the Academy of Arts. She received prestigious commissions from the Medici family.

- Christmas Market: Get into the Christmas spirit in Piazza Santa Croce with the Weihnachtsmarkt, the traditional German Christmas market. Every day, you’ll find stalls selling handmade crafts, local specialties, gift ideas, Christmas decorations and food from all over Europe (10 am–10/11 pm).

- Firenze Marathon: a great opportunity to explore Florence in one breath! Kilometres of beautiful streets and squares steeped in art and history, the speed of the race along the Arno riverbanks, cheering crowds and all the charm and beauty shaped by centuries of human creativity. A dream to experience at the 42nd Estra Firenze Marathon this Sunday!

- Antiques Market: head to Piazza Annigoni, where you'll find plenty of stalls featuring vintage treasures, collectables, books, paintings and all kinds of antiques.

- Comics, Vinyl & Vintage Market: over the weekend, Piazza dei Ciompi will be transformed into a retro paradise, with a must-visit market for lovers of collecting, vintage finds and timeless style (9 am–8 pm).

- Oltrarno Artisan Trail: starting Friday, you'll have the chance to explore local workshops, watch live demonstrations, take part in free workshops and meet the talented artisans and creatives who bring the Oltrarno neighbourhood to life (info: oltrarnopromuove.it).

- Il Salotto del Carmine: on Sunday, Piazza del Carmine will be all about shopping, creativity and rediscovering the charm of the past. Browse stalls featuring handmade crafts, antiques, vintage clothing and local food specialities (8 am–7 pm).

- Firenze JOULUTORI – Nordic Christmas Market: over the weekend, The Social Hub will turn into a little corner of Northern Europe, with Christmas decorations, handmade crafts, design pieces and delicious food specialities (10 am–7 pm).

- Experience Christmas around Florence: Empoli Città del Natale: the town's big Christmas event is back, with spectacular light displays, exciting new attractions and 70 wooden market huts (9 am–8 pm); Oberdrauburg Christmas Market: on Sunday, Signa offers a chance to discover international flavours and enjoy the festive atmosphere through a celebration of different cultures and traditions (10 am–7 pm); Christmas Village in Calenzano: back with 900 square metres of Christmas markets, sweet treats and entertainment. And there's more: you'll also find festive stalls, handmade creations and Christmas magic in Badia a Settimo, Bagno a Ripoli, San Godenzo and beyond.
