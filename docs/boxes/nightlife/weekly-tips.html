Weekly Tips

TIPS FOR THE WEEKEND 31st OCTOBER - 1st NOVEMBER IN FLORENCE




Halloween: there are loads of Halloween events happening this Saturday in Florence and the surrounding area! From a spooky evening surrounded by armour and creepy characters at the Stibbert Museum, to a magical and mysterious experience at Castello dell'Acciaiolo. You can also spend the night exploring mysteries, murders and the stories of some of the world's most notorious criminals at the Serial Killer Museum, or dive into the Dark Circus at Limonaia Strozzi, a dark and eerie circus filled with mysterious creatures, acrobats, creepy characters and spine-chilling sets. And that's not all! For more info: firenzetoday.it

Free Museum Sunday: as on the first Sunday of every month, state museums are free to visit in Florence too! From the Uffizi Galleries to the Bargello National Museum, the Medici Chapels, the Accademia Gallery, the Archaeological Museum and Palazzo Davanzati, there are plenty of places to explore, including some hidden gems waiting to be discovered.

Día de Muertos Parade: on Sunday, a parade celebrating the Day of the Dead tradition will make its way through the city centre, featuring colourful skulls, traditional costumes and Mexican music. It will kick off at 11 am in Piazza Santa Croce and head to Piazza Santa Maria Novella, turning the city streets into a vibrant celebration full of colour, costumes and music.

Museum recommendations: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting Broken, a major exhibition exploring the theme of fragments in the history of sculpture, from archaeology to contemporary art. At the Cattedrale dell'Immagine, you can experience Inferno, an immersive exhibition dedicated to Dante Alighieri. Finally, Villa Bardini is hosting Firenze '50 '60 '70, a photographic journey through three decades of Florence's history, told through images from the Foto Locchi Archive.

The Museo degli Innocenti has just opened a major exhibition dedicated to Artemisia Gentileschi, the first woman admitted to the Academy of Arts. She received prestigious commissions from the Medici family and was part of the same circles as Galileo Galilei and other artists of her time.

Savonarola Antiques Market: this Sunday, Piazza Savonarola will host its antiques, vintage and collectibles market. Browse the stalls for collectibles, vintage items, rare books, paintings, prints, porcelain, antique furniture and plenty of quirky finds that will appeal to both seasoned collectors and curious visitors (8 am-8 pm).

All Saints' Day Fair: the big autumn market is back at Parco delle Cascine this Sunday, with over 300 stalls selling everything from homeware and handmade crafts to local food specialities and gift ideas (8 am-8 pm).

The Kingdom of Pumpkins and Straw: head to Villa Montalvo Park in Campi Bisenzio for an autumn-themed experience filled with pumpkins, giant straw sculptures, shows, workshops and even a whole amusement park! (10 am-8 pm).

Paint and Drink: set in the beautiful surroundings of Villa Strozzi Machiavelli, this is your chance to create your own painting with guidance from artist Enrico Guerrini while sipping a glass of wine. A fun and creative way to spend some time! (info: +39 347 311903).
