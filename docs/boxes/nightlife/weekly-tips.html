Weekly Tips




The Long Weekend 06-07-08th December in Florence


Florence lights up for the holidays with Christmas tree ceremonies, dazzling light installations, and festive markets across the city.
Enjoy free museum access, skate under the stars, and discover unique handmade treasures from local artisans.
From the Firenze Light Festival to traditional crafts in historic piazzas,
this weekend celebrates the magic of the season with creativity, culture, and Florentine charm.
Get ready to explore, shop, and soak in the holiday spirit!



Christmas Tree Lighting - Saturday

The Historical Parade of the Florentine Republic kicks off the festivities
with the lighting of Christmas trees in Piazza del Duomo, Piazza della Repubblica, and Piazza San Firenze.



Sunday at the Museum - Sunday 8th December

Like every first Sunday of the month, state museums are free---even in Florence.
From the Uffizi Galleries and the Bargello National Museum to smaller, lesser-known gems waiting to be discovered.



Firenze Light Festival

Light installations, video mapping, and projections on several buildings throughout the city---
an invitation to enjoy Florence's beauty with care and respect for its heritage.
From Ponte Vecchio to Santa Maria Novella, from Palazzo Vecchio to the illuminated boulevards.



Florence on Ice

The garden at Fortezza da Basso turns into a Christmas Village
with the longest ice-skating rink in Europe and a 55-meter-tall Ferris wheel offering amazing views over the city skyline.



Christmas Market at Piazza Santa Croce

Enjoy the Christmas magic in Piazza Santa Croce with the traditional market.
Every day you'll find stalls with crafts, local products, gift ideas, decorations, and food from all over the world.



International Ceramic Fair

When Christmas meets ceramics you get truly unique gifts.
This weekend in Piazza Ognissanti there's a market-exhibition dedicated to art ceramics,
with artisans from all over Italy showcasing their one-of-a-kind creations.



Fierucola dell'Immacolata

For a Christmas focused on local and sustainable craftsmanship,
over the weekend Piazza SS. Annunziata fills up with flavors, traditions, and handmade creations,
with an emphasis on local artisans and environmental sustainability.



Creative Mani Factory

A weekend at Manifattura Tabacchi celebrating the beauty of local and handmade productions.
Fifty exhibitors from all over Italy will showcase ceramics, textiles, illustrations, jewelry, accessories, and decorations.



The Joy of Christmas - Sunday in Bagno a Ripoli

On Sunday the famous Forte dei Marmi Market arrives "from the sea to Christmas".
At Villa del Padule you'll find a Christmas market filled with creativity and high-quality Italian craftsmanship.



The Artisan

Over the weekend in Piazza dei Ciompi you'll find a market dedicated to artistic and traditional Florentine crafts:
jewelry, wooden and ceramic objects, hand-decorated Christmas ornaments, handmade notebooks and planners,
bags, goldsmithing, fashion, collages, paintings, leather goods, home décor, and illustrations.



Smile Market

At Stazione Leopolda you'll find a charity market with top fashion brands---
clothing, shoes, bags, and accessories from major labels, all new.



Enjoy Market

This weekend in Piazza Indipendenza there's a market focused on vintage and handmade items.



Savonarola Antiquaria - Sunday

On Sunday in Piazza Savonarola you can browse the stalls in search of antiques, rare finds, and vintage treasures.



Arts & Crafts in the Oltrarno - Sunday

On Sunday in Piazza Santo Spirito there will be lots of stalls with crafts, antiques, books, clothing, and vintage pieces,
as well as typical products like honey, truffles, and much more.



Fierucola del Carmine

Stock up on groceries in Piazza del Carmine with products from the "Fierucolanti":
bread, pasta, vegetables, new olive oil, wine, fruit, honey, flours, and traditional cheeses.
