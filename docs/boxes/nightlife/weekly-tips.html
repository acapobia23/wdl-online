Weekly Tips




THE WEEKEND 7 – 8th MARCH IN FLORENCE




MUSEUMS, EVENTS and MARKETS:


International Women’s Day: On Sunday, March 8, the Civic Museums of Florence and Palazzo Medici Riccardi will be open free of charge to all women! Get lost in the huge halls of Palazzo Vecchio, explore the modern art at the Museo Novecento, and don’t miss the Stibbert Museum, Galleria Casamonti, the Zeffirelli Foundation, and more…

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec: The Museo degli Innocenti takes you on an immersive journey into fin-de-siècle Paris, featuring iconic posters, colorful lithographs, and illustrations by the French master Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec.

Oceani. David Doubilet: Villa Bardini and National Geographic celebrate the work of photographer David Doubilet, a pioneer of underwater exploration and one of the world’s leading underwater photographers.

Frida Kahlo, Emotion Exhibition: The Cattedrale dell’Immagine hosts an exclusive immersive exhibition dedicated to the incredible Frida Kahlo, letting visitors dive into the rich and complex universe of the iconic Mexican artist.

Eccellenza di Toscana: Stazione Leopolda is hosting this weekend the 101 top names of Tuscany’s incredible wine heritage – a must for wine lovers!

Visarno Market: Set in the stunning grounds of Ippodromo del Visarno, the spring edition is back with a wide and carefully curated selection of vintage finds, handmade creations, mid-century pieces, illustrations, vinyl records and cool design (10am–7pm).

Fiera dei Quaresimali: The countdown to Easter begins with the traditional Quaresimali fairs, taking over Parco delle Cascine for four Sundays in a row, with hundreds of stalls stretching over a mile and a half (8am–7:30pm).

Demo Brunch & Shop: On Sunday at Palazzo Serristori, enjoy a full day surrounded by vintage, design and handmade pop-ups (10am–6pm).

Arti e Mestieri d’Oltrarno: On Sunday the square comes alive with stands selling crafts, antiques, books, clothing and vintage treasures, plus local goodies like honey and truffles, along with artistic and ethnic handicrafts (9am–7pm).

Festa di Primavera: Sunday in Piazza del Carmine you’ll find the Fierucolanti’s stalls — from artistic crafts to delicious food products — plus workshops and live music (9am–7pm).
