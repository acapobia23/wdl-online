Weekly Tips

THE WEEKEND 28-29th JUNE IN FLORENCE

→ EVENTS / MUSEUMS:
- Open-Air Museum: For breathtaking views of Florence from above and a deeper look into the city’s urban history, you can visit Florence’s towers, gates, and fortresses: Porta San Frediano, Torre della Zecca, Torre San Niccolò, and Fortezza da Basso are all open to the public. (More info at www.musefirenze.it)
- “1925–2025: A Hundred Years of Science in Florence”: A special exhibition marking the 100th anniversary of the founding of the Institute for the History of Science – today’s Galileo Museum – with rare editions of ancient books, archive photos, historical documents, and scientific instruments from the museum’s collection.
- Forte Belvedere: Built at the end of the 1500s as a fortified citadel to defend the Oltrarno side of the city, Forte Belvedere is now open to visitors with a new exhibition telling the story and identity of Florence through the Firenze Forma Continua project (Open daily 10am–8pm).
- Outdoor Cinema: Apriti Cinema: A free summer film series in the courtyard of Palazzo Pitti, with screenings starting at 9:45 PM. (Info and full schedule at www.estatefiorentina.it).
Cinema in Manifattura: “In Cantiere” offers daily screenings of previews, classics, films in original language with Italian subtitles, kids’ movies, and documentaries, along with guest talks and debates at Manifattura Tabacchi. (Tickets and schedule: liveticket.it/stensen).
Cinema nel Chiostro: Every night in the courtyard of the former Sant’Orsola convent, enjoy films in original language thanks to a collaboration with Spazio Alfieri. (Tickets and schedule: santorsola.marte.18tickets.it)

→ MARKETS AND FOOD:
- Tanabata Festival: This weekend at Villa Vogel, celebrate the Japanese "festival of wishes" with events, performances, live shows, and a market featuring traditional Japanese crafts. (Open 5 PM – 11 PM)
- Ars Manualis – The Legacy of Craftsmanship: Head to Piazza Santa Maria Novella this weekend to explore Tuscan artistic craftsmanship at its best. (Open 10 AM – 8 PM)
- Only Usato Market: All weekend at the Ultravox space in the Cascine Park, check out this market dedicated to vintage and second-hand finds — and don’t miss a ride on the 55-meter Ferris wheel for a stunning view of Florence from above!
- Antique Market: In Piazza Annigoni, browse through stands filled with vintage treasures, collectibles, old books, paintings, and all sorts of antiques.
