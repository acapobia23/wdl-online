Weekly Tips

THE WEEKEND 14th - 15th NOVEMBER IN FLORENCE




Museum Tips: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting Broken, a major exhibition exploring the theme of fragments throughout the history of sculpture, from ancient archaeology to contemporary art. At the Cathedral of the Image, you can experience Inferno, an immersive exhibition dedicated to Dante Alighieri. The Museo del Novecento, with its exhibition Stimmung, takes you on a journey through paintings, sculptures, atmospheres and soundscapes, offering a whole new way of experiencing art. And finally, the Museo degli Innocenti is hosting a major exhibition dedicated to Artemisia Gentileschi – the first woman to be admitted to the Academy of Arts. She received prestigious commissions from the Medici family.

Indipendenza Antiquaria: this weekend, Piazza Indipendenza will host 110 stalls selling furniture, books, prints, sculptures, paintings, decorative objects and much more (9 am–7 pm).

Fierucolina di Santo Spirito: on Sunday, head over to this lovely little market to shop for local produce and handmade crafts (9 am–7 pm).

Prim.Olio: extra virgin olive oil takes centre stage at this event in Bagno a Ripoli. Dozens of local producers will be there, giving you the chance to taste and buy their freshly pressed liquid gold (9 am–7 pm).

Christmas Market: get into the Christmas spirit in Piazza Santa Croce with the Weihnachtsmarkt, Florence's traditional German Christmas market. Open daily, with stalls selling handmade crafts, local specialities, gift ideas, Christmas decorations and food from across Europe (10 am–10/11 pm).

ArTour Gusto Toscano: Piazza Strozzi will host a weekend food and wine market celebrating the best of Tuscany, from bread and craft beer to olive oil, coffee and much more.

Bricks in Florence Festival: this weekend, Teatro Cartiere Carrara welcomes back one of Italy's biggest events dedicated to the world of LEGO bricks, with something for fans of all ages (10 am–8 pm).

Librokilo: this weekend at Manifattura Tabacchi, browse thousands of books saved from being pulped, all on sale for just €10 per kilo!

Hand Made – Le Eccellenze Artigiane: set in the beautiful Palazzo Guicciardini, this event is back to celebrate Italian craftsmanship and original handmade creations. Get ready for a weekend dedicated to creativity, beauty and the charm of unique, handcrafted pieces.

Creative Factory: head to Piazza dei Ciompi for an arts and crafts market featuring a variety of independent makers. It's a great opportunity to discover and shop for handmade creations, meet the artists and find out more about their work (10 am–8 pm).

Paint and Drink: in the beautiful setting of Villa Strozzi Machiavelli, you can enjoy a glass of wine while creating your own painting, guided by artist Enrico Guerrini (info: +39 347311903)!
