Weekly Tips

TIPS FOR THE WEEKEND 26-27th SEPTEMBER IN FLORENCE




European Heritage Days: an entire weekend to rediscover museums, libraries and cultural sites, with special openings, guided tours and evening admission for just €1. On Saturday evening, many museums will stay open until 11 pm – including the Uffizi – giving you the chance to admire some of their masterpieces in the beautiful evening light. For info: cultura.gov.it

Antiques Biennale: Palazzo Corsini is hosting the BIAF – Florence International Biennale of Antiques – until October 4, bringing together around 80 galleries showcasing some of the very best of the Italian and international art market

Green Florence: over 2,000 square metres of natural grass and centuries-old olive trees will transform Piazza Santa Croce into a little green oasis, with Tuscan food specialties, tastings and live music

Corri la Vita: on Sunday, this popular charity run brings together sport, culture and solidarity, starting at the Cascine Park and finishing in Piazza della Signoria

Broken. The Power of the Fragment: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting a major exhibition exploring the role of fragments in the history of sculpture, from archaeology to contemporary art. The show brings together fascinating works ranging from ancient Egypt to Michelangelo, from classical Greece to Rodin, as well as contemporary artists such as Louise Bourgeois, Francesco Vezzoli and Danh Vo

FloReMus: the final weekend of this international festival dedicated to Renaissance music, featuring some of Europe's leading early-music performers, alongside young musicians and events in historic locations such as Palazzo Medici Riccardi, the Cenacolo del Fuligno and Florence's libraries

Fabbrica Europa: running until October 11, this international festival of contemporary performing arts brings together theatre, dance and music. For info: fabbricaeuropa.net

Rare Market: the vintage and handmade market is back at the Visarno Racecourse, celebrating all things rare and handcrafted. Expect a journey through different eras, with designer jewellery, vintage bags, contemporary creations and collectible vinyl records

Vintage & Collectibles Fair: the regular weekend market is back in Piazza dei Ciompi, with comics, vinyl records and vintage clothing (H 9/20)

Antiques Market: this weekend, Piazza dei Ciompi hosts a market dedicated to vintage finds, collectibles and pop culture – perfect for enthusiasts, curious visitors and collectors looking for unique pieces and retro treasures (H 9/19)
