Weekly Tips

WEEKEND VIBES IN FLORENCE — June 22–23 (Curated by Fuordarno’s Carolina Troni)

→ EVENTS & CULTURE:

Apriti Cinema: Florence’s summer open-air cinema is back at Palazzo Pitti, with free evening screenings starting at 9:45 PM. Check the full program at www.estatefiorentina.it

Discover Kyoto in Florence: Celebrating 60 years of twinning between Florence and Tokyo, a unique exhibition opens at the Duomo Auditorium showcasing Japanese craftsmanship, philosophy, and material culture (10 AM – 5 PM).

Toscana Pride: On Saturday, the vibrant rainbow wave of Toscana Pride fills the streets of Prato in support of LGBTQIA+ rights, women, and migrant communities (starts at 4 PM).

Cetilar Night Run & Marzocco Coronation: Festivities for Florence’s patron saint, San Giovanni, kick off early this year. On Saturday at 9 PM, a 9.9 km nighttime run starts from Piazza Duomo and loops through the historic center. On Sunday at 11 AM in Piazza della Signoria, don’t miss the traditional coronation of the Marzocco at Palazzo Vecchio, with a historical parade and flag-throwers from the Uffizi.

→ MARKETS & FOOD:

Santo Spirito in Fiera: Sunday brings a lovely artisan and vintage market to the piazza, packed with handmade goods, antiques, and tasty treats (8 AM – 8 PM).

Creative Factory: Piazza Santissima Annunziata transforms into a vibrant design showcase, featuring jewelry, fashion, illustrations, ceramics, home décor, and more by emerging Italian artists (9:30 AM – 8 PM).

Collectibles in Florence: At the Teatro Cartiere Carrata parking lot, browse vintage, modern design, and quirky curiosities all weekend long (10 AM – 7 PM).

Comics & More: Piazza dei Ciompi hosts a weekend market of comics, records, CDs, and retro treasures (9 AM – 8 PM).

Lebanon Food Festival: Head to the Limonaia of Villa Strozzi to experience the rich flavors and aromas of Middle Eastern cuisine—an immersive Lebanese culinary journey.

International Yoga Day: Celebrate balance and wellness with a range of events across the city, from the Conventino Letterario to the Parco dell’Anconella.
