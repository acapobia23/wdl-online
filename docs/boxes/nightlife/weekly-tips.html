Weekly Tips

THE WEEKEND 21 – 22th NOVEMBER IN FLORENCE




- Museum Tips: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting Broken, a major exhibition exploring the theme of fragments throughout the history of sculpture, from ancient archaeology to contemporary art. At the Cathedral of the Image, you can experience Inferno, an immersive exhibition dedicated to Dante Alighieri. The Museo del Novecento, with its exhibition Stimmung, takes you on a journey through paintings, sculptures, atmospheres and soundscapes, offering a whole new way of experiencing art. And finally, the Museo degli Innocenti is hosting a major exhibition dedicated to Artemisia Gentileschi – the first woman to be admitted to the Academy of Arts. She received prestigious commissions from the Medici family.

- Christmas Market: get into the Christmas spirit in Piazza Santa Croce with the Weihnachtsmarkt, the traditional German Christmas market. Every day, you’ll find stalls selling handmade crafts, local specialties, gift ideas, Christmas decorations and food from all over Europe (10 am–10/11 pm).

- Santo Spirito Fair: every Sunday, Piazza Santo Spirito fills up with stalls selling handmade crafts, antiques, books, clothes, vintage finds, as well as delicious local food and traditional products (9 am–7 pm).

- Car Bazar: every Sunday at the Visarno Hippodrome, check out this quirky flea market where car trunks become little market stalls! Expect vintage finds, creative pieces, second-hand treasures and plenty of bargains (10 am–7 pm).

- Christmas Vintage Market: The Social Hub Florence hosts Pimp My Vintage in its Christmas edition: a full day dedicated to vintage, handmade crafts and sustainable shopping, with unique clothes and accessories, vinyl records, jewellery, design pieces and handmade creations (11 am–8 pm).

- Only Usato Popup Boutique: on Via della Chiesa, in the heart of Santo Spirito, enjoy a weekend dedicated to second-hand fashion and carefully selected vintage pieces, each with its own story to tell.

- Japanese Festival: in Scandicci, at Piazzale della Resistenza, spend the weekend discovering Japanese culture, traditional arts, dance, food and special encounters between Italy and Japan (11 am–8 pm).

- OLEA: head to Montespertoli for its extra virgin olive oil festival! A great chance to taste freshly pressed olive oil, meet the local producers and discover how it’s made.

- Paint and Drink: in the beautiful setting of Villa Strozzi Machiavelli, you can enjoy a glass of wine while creating your own painting, guided by artist Enrico Guerrini (info: +39 347311903)!
