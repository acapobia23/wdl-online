Weekly Tips

TIPS FOR THE WEEKEND 16 - 18th OCTOBER IN FLORENCE




MUSEUM RECOMMENDATIONS:

Broken: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting a major exhibition exploring the theme of fragments in the history of sculpture, from archaeology to contemporary art. At the Cattedrale dell'Immagine, you can experience Inferno, an immersive exhibition dedicated to Dante Alighieri. Finally, Villa Bardini is hosting Firenze '50 '60 '70, a photographic journey through three decades of Florence's history, told through images from the Foto Locchi Archive. Finally, Palazzo Medici Riccardi is hosting Beyond the Renaissance: Between Dürer and Parmigianino, an exhibition dedicated to European Mannerist prints and to the deep connection contemporary artist Georg Baselitz, one of the leading figures in today's art scene, has developed with the masters of the 16th century.

MARKETS:

Piantala: the biggest plant pop-up is coming to Manifattura Tabacchi from Friday to Sunday, with over 15,000 plants across more than 200 different varieties! There'll also be yoga, DJ sets, a small market and workshops (H 10/20)

Ars Manualis - The Heritage of Craftsmanship: starting Friday, Piazza Santa Maria Novella will host an arts and crafts market celebrating the very best of traditional Italian craftsmanship (H 10/20)

Ognissanti in Festa: head to Piazza Ognissanti on Friday and Saturday for a market dedicated to vintage finds and creative crafts. Expect unique treasures and one-of-a-kind pieces, along with a nostalgic journey through handmade creativity (H 9/20)

Indipendenza Antiquaria: this weekend, Piazza Indipendenza will be filled with 110 stalls selling furniture, books, prints, sculptures, paintings, antiques and all sorts of curiosities. A wonderful trip back in time for vintage lovers and treasure hunters! (H 9/19)

SAM - Sant'Ambrogio Made: on Sunday, explore Florence's Sant'Ambrogio neighbourhood and enjoy a day filled with contemporary crafts, workshops, art and local shops opening their doors (H 9/19)

Fierucola di Santo Spirito: on Sunday, head to Piazza Santo Spirito to shop for organic farm produce and handmade crafts (H 9/19)

Car Bazar: on Sunday, the market returns to the Visarno Hippodrome! Here, exhibitors use their car boots as market stalls, selling vintage finds, creative items, second-hand treasures and all sorts of things they're ready to part with (H 10/18)

Wine tasting and grape harvest celebrations: on Sunday, explore the surrounding hills and stop by the Grape Harvest Festival in San Casciano, Borgo DiVino in Montaione, or the Autumn Festival in Scandicci. A great way to enjoy the local wine and celebrate the harvest season!

Chestnut festivals: October is here, autumn is in full swing, and it's the perfect time to enjoy some delicious local chestnuts! Head to the festivals in Marradi, San Piero a Sieve, Firenzuola, Borgo San Lorenzo, Scarperia or Carmignano for a taste of autumn and some lovely local traditions

The Kingdom of Pumpkins and Straw: until November 1st, the autumn season returns to Parco di Villa Montalvo in Campi Bisenzio, with giant pumpkins, huge straw sculptures, shows, workshops and a whole amusement park (H 10/20)
