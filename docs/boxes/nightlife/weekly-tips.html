Weekly Tips




THE WEEKEND 21 - 22nd MARCH IN FLORENCE




MUSEUMS, EVENTS and MARKETS:


Giornate FAI: The most important public event dedicated to the valorization of Italian cultural and landscape heritage, where you can visit historical places, military institutes, villages, and treasures normally closed to the public. Reservations at: www.fondoambiente.it

Rothko in Florence: The Fondazione Palazzo Strozzi is presenting an unmissable exhibition dedicated to the great American art master Mark Rothko. The show explores the evolution of his work: from his early figurative paintings, influenced by Expressionism and Surrealism, to the famous abstract canvases of the 1950s and 1960s, also highlighting his connection with the Italian artistic tradition.

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec: The Museo degli Innocenti takes you on an immersive journey into fin-de-siècle Paris, featuring iconic posters, colorful lithographs, and illustrations by the French master Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec.

Oceani. David Doubilet: Villa Bardini and National Geographic celebrate the work of photographer David Doubilet, a pioneer of underwater exploration and one of the world's leading underwater photographers.

Frida Kahlo, Emotion Exhibition: The Cattedrale dell'Immagine hosts an exclusive immersive exhibition dedicated to the incredible Frida Kahlo, letting visitors dive into the rich and complex universe of the iconic Mexican artist.


Botanica Urbana: This weekend, Manifattura Tabacchi is hosting a plant and flower market with a selection of exhibitors, talks, labs, and workshops for families, enthusiasts, adults, and kids (9am–7pm).

Enjoy Market: In Piazza dei Ciompi you'll find a vintage and handmade market with jewelry, clothes, bags, accessories, home decor, gift ideas, and antiques (9am–7pm).

Wonder and Pick: In Scandicci and Lastra a Signa you can wander through fields of tulips — over 150,000 blooms across 65 varieties — and even pick your own.

Fiorinfiera: The flower market returns to the central streets of Sesto Fiorentino for a full-on spring weekend.

Only Usato: On Sunday at Conventino Letterario there's an event all about reuse and second-hand finds — clothes, accessories, vinyl, books, plus workshops, food, music, and more (11am–8pm).

Fiera dei Quaresimali: The countdown to Easter kicks off with the Quaresimali fairs, filling the Cascine for four Sundays with hundreds of stalls stretching over 1.5 km (8am–7:30pm).

Santo Spirito in Fiera: On Sunday you can shop for crafts, food products, wellness items, small antiques, clothing, costume jewelry, and much more (9am–7pm).
